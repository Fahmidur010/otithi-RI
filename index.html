Experiences with hosts

Share music, forest paths, and village life with host communities across Bangladesh.

  • Sundarbans village journey

    Journey through riverside villages and discover life along the Sundarbans’ waterways. Meet local communities, take part in Cha Chokro, and listen to storytelling sessions led by the Gunin.

  • Sylhet cultural performance

    Join a community-led cultural performance among the tea gardens — music, dance, and hospitality that welcomes guests into Sylhet’s living traditions.

  • Madhupur forest welcome

    Walk shaded forest paths with local hosts in Madhupur — a quieter pace of travel shaped by community hospitality, nature, and shared time on the trail.
